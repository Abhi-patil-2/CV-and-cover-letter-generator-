CV format guide

European (Europass) — Blue header bar, 2-column layout, photo placeholder, structured sections. Current official EU standard. Best for Germany, France, Netherlands, Scandinavia, EU jobs.

American (ATS) — Clean single-column, bold section headings, achievement bullets with metrics, keyword-dense. No photo or DOB. Highest ATS pass rate. Best for US, Canada, UK, global MNCs.

Indian General — Career objective, personal details table, declaration. Standard across BFSI, government, general corporate India.

Indian IT — Technical skills matrix, project tables with tech stack. Preferred by TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL, Indian startups and IT MNCs.

Indian MBA — Education prominent, leadership roles, internships, extra-curriculars. Best for consulting, FMCG, banking management roles.
