How to use

Step 1 — Add your profile
Go to Profile tab, paste your resume or bio, tap Save. Done once, remembered always.

Step 2 — Paste the job description
Paste in any language. Hit Detect or pick the language manually.

Step 3 — Pick your CV style
European for EU roles, American for US/global, Indian variants for Indian companies.

Step 4 — Generate and download
Tap Generate. Download each document as a named PDF. History is tracked automatically.

CV style guide

European — Europass-inspired. Formal, dates on left, clean layout. Best for Germany, France, Netherlands, Scandinavia.

American — ATS keyword-heavy. Achievement bullets with metrics, no photo or DOB. Best for US, Canada, UK startups.

Indian General — Career objective, personal details, declaration section. Works across sectors in India.

Indian IT — Technical skills matrix upfront, project details with tech stack, certifications. Best for TCS, Infosys, Wipro, startups.

Indian MBA — Education prominent, leadership roles, internships, extra-curriculars. Best for management and consulting roles.

Language support

Paste a JD in any language — German, French, Spanish, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Chinese and more. Tap Detect to auto-identify the language, or select manually from the dropdown. All output documents are always generated in English.
