CV format guide

European (Europass) — Blue header, 2-column feel, structured sections. Best for EU jobs.

American (ATS) — Keyword-dense, achievement bullets with metrics. Best for US/Canada/UK.

Indian General — Objective, personal details, declaration. Works across all Indian sectors.

Indian IT — Skills matrix, project tables. Best for TCS, Infosys, Wipro, startups.

Indian MBA — Education prominent, leadership, internships. Best for management roles.
