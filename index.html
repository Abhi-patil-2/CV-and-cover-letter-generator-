How to use

Step 1 — Add your profile
Go to "My Profile" and paste your resume or bio. Save it once — it stays on your device.

Step 2 — Paste the job description
Copy the full job posting and paste it in the Generate tab.

Step 3 — Pick your style
European for EU companies, American for US/international roles. Both is fine too.

Step 4 — Generate & download
Tap "Generate documents" and download each as a PDF.

Tips for best results

• Include achievements with numbers in your profile (e.g. "increased sales by 30%")
• Paste the complete job description including company description
• You can regenerate multiple times — each output is different
• Profile data stays only on your device, never shared

Privacy

Your profile is stored in your browser's localStorage — it never leaves your device except when you click "Generate", at which point it is sent to the Anthropic AI API to produce your documents.
